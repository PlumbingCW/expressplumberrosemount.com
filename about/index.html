Rosemount Express Plumbing works out of 14845 South Robert Trail in Rosemount. We handle emergency calls and planned work for homes and businesses in Rosemount, Apple Valley, Farmington, Inver Grove Heights and Hastings.

We are licensed and insured. Minnesota requires plumbing contractors to be licensed through the state Department of Labor and Industry, and you can look up any plumber's license on the state lookup before you let them in the house.

What we promise, and what we do not

We promise a clear price before work starts, same-day appointments when available, and a phone line that is answered 24/7. We do not promise an exact arrival time, because traffic, weather and the jobs ahead of yours decide that, and a promise we cannot keep helps nobody.

We also tell you when a repair is not worth it. A twenty-year-old tank with a rusted base is a replacement conversation, and a boiler with a cracked section usually is too.

Why local knowledge matters

Rosemount water comes from city wells in the Jordan aquifer, and the city lists its hardness at 17 grains per gallon. The sewer system is run by the city, with treatment by the Metropolitan Council. Knowing who owns which pipe saves time on every call.

The towns around us each have their own water and rules, which is why the service areas page gives each one its own page.

The licensing standard we work to

Minnesota's Department of Labor and Industry requires a plumbing contractor license for businesses that contract plumbing work. The license depends on a responsible master plumber, insurance and a surety bond, and the state keeps a public license lookup so you can verify who you are dealing with.

We are licensed and insured, and we expect to be checked. If a plumber at your door cannot tell you which license covers the work, that is worth a second call to the state before you sign anything.

How we talk about price

Plumbing prices depend on access, materials, the condition of what is already there and the time of day. We do not post price lists on this site, because a number without a look at your house would be a guess dressed up as a fact.

What we do commit to is the order of events. You hear a clear price before work starts. If the plumber finds something that changes the job, the new price is explained and agreed before any extra work begins.

Serving the towns around Rosemount

Dispatch covers Rosemount, Apple Valley, Farmington, Inver Grove Heights and Hastings. Each town buys its water and runs its sewers differently, so a call from Hastings starts with different questions than a call from Apple Valley.

The Apple Valley, Farmington, Inver Grove Heights and Hastings pages lay out those local differences. If you are outside those towns, call and describe the address, and we will tell you straight whether we can come.

When to call 911 instead

Call 911 for anything that puts people in danger, including a gas smell you cannot trace, carbon monoxide alarms, or water pouring near live electrical equipment while you cannot reach the shutoff. Leave the building first, then call from outside.

For a plumbing emergency with no immediate danger, the dispatch line is the right call, and the first minute on the phone covers what to shut off and what to leave alone.
