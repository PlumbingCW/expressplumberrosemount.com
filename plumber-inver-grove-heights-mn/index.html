Why Inver Grove Heights Water Marks Pipes and Valves

The city draws from six wells, 360 to 542 feet deep, in the Jordan aquifer. Its 2024 water report puts hardness at 20 to 22 grains per gallon, or 370 parts per million. The city says that is high enough that most homes and businesses soften their water, either with their own equipment or through a service.

That makes softener hardware part of many plumbing calls here. The same report says low pressure and sand in the water are most often caused by a malfunctioning softener, and gives system pressure of about 75 psi. Before you assume a pipe has failed, check the softener bypass and tell us what the unit is doing.

Unsoftened taps also scale. Mineral buildup narrows aerators, coats shower valves and shortens the life of small valves. If a fixture is weak or the spray is uneven, we look at the softener first, then at the fixture. For hidden loss behind a wall, our leak detection service locates the source without opening more drywall than needed.

Backup in the House or at the Street Connection

A stoppage is either inside your walls or out at the private line. Knowing which side changes the fix.

Likely inside the house.

  • Only one fixture is slow or backs up.
  • Gurgling when a nearby drain runs.
  • A snake or plunger clears it briefly.
  • A drain trap or branch line is the cause.

Likely in the lateral or main.

  • Several drains back up at once.
  • Basement floor drain or lowest tub rises first.
  • Water comes up when the washer drains.
  • A camera shows roots, grease or a collapsed section.

The first case often needs a snake. The second may need our hydro jetting service, which uses high-pressure water to clear grease, scale and roots from the line, followed by a camera check. Our sewer line cleaning page explains the lateral side in more detail.

Sump Discharge and the Sanitary Sewer

The city's water report FAQ is plain: sump pumps that collect groundwater are not allowed to discharge into the sanitary sewer, and hoses must route outside the home. The city plan adds a goal of checking that sump pumps and building drains are not tied into the sewer when permits are pulled.

That affects a replacement pump. If the old discharge ends in a floor drain, a new pump just repeats the violation. We check where the water goes and route it outside, then confirm the check valve is on the correct side. See our sump pump service for what the work involves.

During storms, the pump runs hardest and the city's sewer flows peak, so a misrouted line is a real problem. If your pit is rising and the pump will not start, call the emergency line and keep the pit area clear.
