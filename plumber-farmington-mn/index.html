Water Is Spreading in Your Farmington Home: Do This First

Water damage grows fast because the water keeps running while people search for the cause. These steps limit the damage while the line connects you to dispatch.

  • 1
    Shut the main water valve.Turn it fully clockwise. If you cannot find it, say so on the call and we will talk you through the search.
  • 2
    Cut power near standing water.If water reaches outlets, appliances or the panel, do not step in. Switch off power only from a dry spot, or leave it and call the utility.
  • 3
    Open a low faucet.After the main is off, opening the lowest tap drains pressure from the pipes and slows a leak above it.
  • 4
    Move what you can.Lift rugs, boxes and electronics out of the wet area. Take photos before you move things, because they help later with an insurance claim.
  • 5
    Call and describe the source.Tell the dispatcher where the water starts, what has been shut off and whether any sewage is involved. A plain description sorts a same-day visit from a call that can wait for morning.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call from outside. Do not touch switches or appliances.

Very Hard Water and the Tank in Your Basement

Farmington draws its water from seven city wells, 424 to 512 feet deep per the city's 2025 report, in the Prairie du Chien-Jordan and Jordan aquifers. The city publishes hardness at about 16 grains per gallon. It lists 15.8 grains (271.51 mg/L) on its drinking water report page and 283 mg/L on a separate hardness page, and neither figure carries a report year.

The takeaway is simple even with fuzzy numbers: this is very hard water. Scale forms on heating elements and in the bottom of tanks, and a tank that has run for years without a drain can be coated in it. Rumbling, slow recovery and lukewarm showers are the usual signs, and a failing tank may be a replacement job rather than a repair.

The city's own water page lists pros and cons of softening, including corrosion of pipes, added sodium and about 5 percent water waste. If your hot water fails and you have a softener, tell us, because a bypass valve or brine problem can look like a bad tank. For the full process, see our water heater replacement service and the repair option when a tank can still be saved.

Sump Pumps in Farmington: Where the Water Is Allowed to Go

Farmington city ordinance prohibits property owners from sending clear water to the sanitary sewer. The sewer page names roof water, surface or ground sump pump water, foundation drains and swimming pools. A pump that empties into a floor drain or a laundry sink is a violation and a poor setup, because it sends storm water into a system built for household waste.

That rule shapes how we look at a failed pump. A new pump is only part of the fix if the discharge line ends in the wrong place. We check where the water goes, that the line runs outside and that the check valve is on the correct side, then explain what we found. Our sump pump service covers replacement and the discharge route.

Storms are when pumps fail, and that is exactly when the line is busiest. If the pit is rising and the pump is silent, unplug it and call the emergency line. Keep any wet-dry vacuum running at the pit edge until we arrive.

Who Owns Which Pipe: The Line From House to Street

Farmington's Sewer Services page is direct: the sewer line from a home to the city sewer main is the property owner's responsibility. If a blockage is in that private line, the owner schedules and pays for the work, and the city notes that many local plumbers provide this service. Blockages in the city main are a different matter and belong to Public Works.

The city also maintains its collection system, 89 miles of it, cleaning and televising each of nine sections every seven years. That is useful context when several neighbors back up at once, because it points to the main rather than your pipe. When only your house is affected, the cause is usually on your side of the connection.

Two of our services fit that boundary. Our sewer line cleaning clears the private run, and a camera shows whether roots, grease or a broken section caused the stoppage. A leak detection visit finds hidden water loss when the cause is not obvious.
